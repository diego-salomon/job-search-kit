## Rules for everything written as me
- First person, plain, concrete. Real numbers, real names of tools and companies.
- No em dashes.
- No corporate filler: leverage, passionate, dynamic, seamless, robust, synergy, results-driven, excited to apply, I am writing to apply.
- Show, don't label: say what I did and let the reader conclude.
- Vary sentence length. Read it out loud; if I wouldn't say it, rewrite it.
